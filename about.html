Academic Narrative

Khandakar Rashedul Islam is an environmental scientist dedicated to mitigating emerging global contaminants. He earned his MS and B.Sc. in Environmental Science from Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), focusing his thesis research on microplastics and biochar-based soil amendments.

Working as a Research Assistant at the Asia Arsenic Network and the JUST Laboratory of Environmental Science and Technology, he has developed strong proficiency in advanced analytical instrumentation, including FTIR, SEM, ICP-OES, and GC-MS. His research integrates laboratory analysis with machine learning and multivariate approaches to model pollutant behavior in marine, agricultural, and urban ecosystems.
