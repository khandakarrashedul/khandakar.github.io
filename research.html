Research Program

Investigation of Emerging Pollutants & Sustainable Remediation

1. Microplastics Pollution

Quantifying microplastic occurrence, spatial distribution, polymer identification, and risk indices across coastal beaches, agricultural soils, aquaculture ponds, and food products.

2. Biochar Remediation

Synthesizing biochar and bio-nano composites from agricultural waste (poultry litter, rice husk) and plastic waste (PET bottle carbonized char) for soil amendment and water purification.

3. Wastewater Treatment

Evaluating kinetic, isotherm, and thermodynamic sorption behavior for removing hazardous dyes (Methylene Blue, Acid Red 27) and heavy metals ($Cd^{2+}, Pb^{2+}, Cu^{2+}, Zn^{2+}$).
